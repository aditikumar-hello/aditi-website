Mathematics

From competition mathematics to exploring higher-level ideas, mathematics has been one of my favorite ways to challenge myself.

My Mathematical Journey

Competitive Mathematics

I began studying competitive mathematics in middle school and have continued exploring problems in algebra, geometry, number theory, combinatorics, and probability.

Competitions have taught me not only how to solve problems quickly, but also how to approach unfamiliar problems creatively.

Beyond Competition

More recently, I've become interested in the ideas behind the problems rather than simply solving them as quickly as possible.

I've been exploring topics including probability, graph theory, linear algebra, and Markov chains.

Euler Circle

Through Euler Circle, I've had the opportunity to study mathematics beyond the standard high school curriculum.

Studying Markov chains has introduced me to mathematical ideas such as transition matrices, stationary distributions, eigenvalues, and eigenvectors.

Topics I Enjoy

Number Theory

Divisibility, modular arithmetic, Diophantine equations, and the structures hidden inside integers.

Combinatorics

Counting, probability, graph theory, and finding clever ways to break complicated problems into manageable pieces.

Probability & Markov Chains

Random processes, expected values, stationary distributions, and understanding how systems evolve over time.

What's Next?

I'm continuing to explore mathematics beyond the standard high school curriculum and hope to keep learning through challenging problems, courses, and independent study.
