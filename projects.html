EpiShield

A bioengineering project exploring a polymer-based approach to extending the shelf life of epinephrine auto-injectors.

Our team investigated how materials such as polyvinylpyrrolidone could help protect epinephrine from environmental factors that contribute to degradation. We ended up with the concept of an epinephrine autoinjector that lasted 4 years. We presented our research at the UCLA NanoSystems Institute.
