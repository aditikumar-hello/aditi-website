Projects

A collection of projects I've worked on across mathematics, science, technology, and education.

EpiShield

A bioengineering project exploring a polymer-based approach to extending the shelf life of epinephrine auto-injectors.

Our team investigated how materials such as polyvinylpyrrolidone could help protect epinephrine from environmental factors that contribute to degradation.

Math, For Real

A student-led nonprofit focused on making mathematics education accessible to students through free tutoring and educational resources.

I help teach mathematics and contribute to the development of educational materials, including preparation for competitions such as AMC 8 and AMC 10.

Competitive Programming

I enjoy using programming to solve algorithmic problems and explore computational approaches to problem solving.

My interests include algorithms, data structures, and competitive programming.
