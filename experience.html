Experience

A collection of past experiences and opportunities I worked on.

EpiShield

A bioengineering project exploring a polymer-based approach to extending the shelf life of epinephrine auto-injectors.

Our team investigated how materials such as polyvinylpyrrolidone could help protect epinephrine from environmental factors that contribute to degradation. We ended up with the concept of an epinephrine autoinjector that lasted 4 years. We presented our research in the form of a business plan at the UCLA NanoSystems Institute.

Markov Chains

A summer course at Euler's Circle I took during summer 2026 where I was exposed to theoretical and research-based math.

i studied markov chains through euler circle, exploring stochastic processes, random walks, stationary distributions, recurrence, and the mathematical structure behind systems that evolve randomly over time. the course also introduced me to linear algebra and probability from a more theoretical perspective.

UCSD Biomechanics Course

I learned about the fundamentals of biomechanics and bioinformatics during a one-week course at UCSD during summer 2026.

i studied biomechanics and mechanobiology through a course at uc san diego, exploring how mechanical forces interact with biological systems. the course introduced me to concepts in cellular mechanics, tissue behavior, and the ways physical forces influence biological processes, connecting my interests in biology, engineering, and applied mathematics.
