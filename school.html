Math, For Real

A student-led nonprofit focused on making mathematics education accessible to students through free tutoring and educational resources.

As the vice president of the nonprofit, I handle outreach, new hires, curriculum, and events. Additionally, I help teach mathematics and contribute to the development of educational materials, including preparation for competitions such as AMC 8 and AMC 10. We are currently looking to partner with local community centers, libraries, and elementary schools.

Competitive Programming

I enjoy using programming to solve algorithmic problems and explore computational approaches to problem solving.

My interests include algorithms, data structures, and competitive programming. I am the treasurer of the Competitive Programming Club at my school. Additionally, I was an ACSL finalist in 2026.
